# Funding bounties

Bounty.new uses escrow to protect both creators and solvers. Here's how to fund your bounties and manage payments.

## How escrow works

When you create a bounty:

1. Funds locked - Your payment is held in escrow
2. Work delivered - Solver submits their solution
3. You approve - Review and approve the work
4. Instant payout - Funds released immediately to solver

This protects both parties:
- Solvers know the money is there
- Creators only pay for approved work

## Adding payment methods

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to payment settings">
    Go to your organization's settings page at bounty.new/[slug]/settings/payments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add payment method">
    Choose your preferred payment method:
    - Credit/debit card (instant)
    - ACH bank transfer (1-2 business days)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your account">
    Complete verification to enable payments. Required for security and compliance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start creating bounties">
    Once verified, you can fund bounties and start receiving solutions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Funding a bounty

When creating a bounty:

1. Set reward amount - Choose how much you'll pay for the solution
2. Add to escrow - Funds are immediately locked in escrow
3. Post bounty - Your bounty goes live with guaranteed payment

<Note>
  Funds in escrow are protected and can only be released when you approve work or cancel the bounty.
</Note>

## Managing bounty funds

### Approving work

When a solver submits a solution:

1. Review submission - Check if it meets your requirements
2. Request changes - Ask for revisions if needed
3. Approve - Release funds instantly to the solver

### Canceling a bounty

If you need to cancel:

1. Navigate to the bounty
2. Click Cancel bounty
3. Funds return to your account immediately

<Warning>
  If work has been submitted, communicate with the solver before canceling. Consider partial payment for partial work.
</Warning>

## Dispute resolution

If there's a disagreement:

1. Communicate - Try to resolve directly with the solver
2. Request mediation - Contact grim@bounty.new
3. Review process - Our team reviews the work and requirements
4. Decision - Funds released based on fair assessment

## Payment security

Your payment information is protected:

- Encrypted storage - All payment data is encrypted
- PCI compliance - We follow industry security standards
- Escrow protection - Funds only released when you approve
- Fraud prevention - Advanced security measures protect your account

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Payment method declined">
    - Verify card details are correct
    - Check sufficient funds available
    - Contact your bank if issues persist
    - Try alternative payment method
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Verification issues">
    - Ensure all information matches your ID exactly
    - Use a clear photo of your ID
    - Check that your address is current
    - Contact grim@bounty.new for help
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Funds not released">
    - Confirm you approved the work
    - Check transaction history in settings
    - Allow 1-2 minutes for processing
    - Contact support if funds aren't released
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
