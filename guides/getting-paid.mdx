## Setting up payouts with Stripe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to payment settings">
    Go to bounty.new/settings/payments and click the "Connect with Stripe" button.

    ![Bounty Settings Payments](/images/bounty-settings-payments.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your country">
    A popup will appear asking where you're based. Select your country so Stripe can provide the proper setup details.

    ![Location](/images/location.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign into Stripe Express">
    Enter a new or existing email address to create or access your Stripe Express account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter phone number">
    Provide your phone number for account verification.

    ![Phone number entry](/images/stripe-phone-number.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose business type">
    Stripe will ask about your business. Most people should choose "Individual" unless you're setting this up for a registered business.

    ![Email](/images/email.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill out personal details">
    Complete the personal information form as prompted by Stripe.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your business">
    When asked about your industry and products:

    - **Industry**: Select "Software"
    - **Products/Services**: Enter "Solving bounties on bounty.new" (instead of providing a website)

    This tells Stripe exactly what you're doing and ensures smooth approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete verification">
    Finish any remaining verification steps. Once approved, you'll receive instant payouts when bounties are approved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How payouts work

### Instant release

When a creator approves your solution:

1. Immediate release - Funds are released from escrow instantly
2. Automatic transfer - Money goes directly to your verified payout method
3. No waiting - Get paid immediately, no holding periods

### Payout timeline

## Managing your earnings

### View payout history

Track all your earnings:

1. Go to bounty.new/[slug]/settings/payments
2. View transaction history
3. See pending and completed payouts
4. Download statements for taxes

### Tax information

<Warning>
  You're responsible for reporting bounty earnings on your taxes. Keep records of all payouts for tax filing.
</Warning>

For US-based solvers earning over \$600/year:

- You'll receive a 1099 form
- Report earnings as self-employment income
- Track expenses related to bounty work

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Payout not received">
    - Check your payout method is verified
    - ACH bank transfers take 1-2 business days
    - Verify bank account details are correct
    - Contact [grim@bounty.new](mailto:grim@bounty.new) for help
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Verification issues">
    - Ensure all information matches your ID exactly
    - Use a clear photo of your ID
    - Check that your address is current
    - Contact support if verification fails
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Wrong payout method">
    - Update your payout method in settings
    - New method applies to future payouts
    - Previous payouts can't be redirected
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
